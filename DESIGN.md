# Console workbench

A tactile, full-viewport recreation of a late-1970s PC-50x setup. Follow .impeccable/direction.md. Ivory wings, charcoal central case, orange ten-key bank, black swappable cartridges, black-and-white television. The screen dominates at left and the console occupies the right side. The shelf is inventory, not a marketing-card grid.

Typography: self-hosted Obel Mono and Inter. Colors, space, motion and type are defined in web/tokens.css.
