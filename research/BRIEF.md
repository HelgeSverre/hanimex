# Hanimex preservation and emulation research

Date: 2026-11-09

## Objective
Identify Helge's childhood Hanimex “625-B RPG” cartridge Pong console, find public dumps or explain whether ROM dumps apply, and establish an evidence-based path to emulation in Nim or Rust.

## Questions
1. Is 625-B a model number, television standard, or a label shared by multiple models?
2. Which cartridge family and silicon does the photographed machine use?
3. Do cartridges contain executable ROM, fixed game logic, or both?
4. Are dumps, die photographs, schematics, manuals, or working emulators publicly available?
5. What can be implemented faithfully now, and what needs hardware measurements?

## Research tracks
- Exact-name identification and original-owner photographs.
- Hardware, cartridge chips, manufacturer documentation and family compatibility.
- Existing emulation, preservation artifacts, ROM/dump searches and implementation feasibility.

## Evidence rules
Prefer manufacturer documentation, photographed hardware, and original emulator source. Mark observed facts, inference and unknowns separately. A failed search does not prove an artifact does not exist. Record links and implementation limits. Do not call a generic Pong recreation a hardware emulator.
